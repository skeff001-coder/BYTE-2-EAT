Restoring a purchase

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. This will restore any scan packs linked to your Apple ID.

Managing or cancelling

Scan packs are one-time purchases, not subscriptions — there's nothing to cancel. To review your purchase history, go to your Apple ID Account Settings > Purchase History.

Privacy & Terms

You can find our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions inside the app under Settings > Legal.
